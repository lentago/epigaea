Kitchen Speaker Dial

The round Hue dial runs the kitchen Sonos. Turn the ring for the kitchen's volume: clockwise is louder, a fast spin jumps more, and all the way down is silent. There is no mute button.

Button Tap once Tap twice
Play / pause Next song
Volume 20% 20% for every room on this floor playing along
Volume 30% 30% for every room on this floor playing along
Volume 40% 40% for every room on this floor playing along

Hold a second, then let go: the kitchen joins the music already playing in the dining room or the office. Hold it again and the kitchen leaves; that room plays on.

  • Nothing playing there? Holding does nothing. Both rooms playing? The kitchen joins the dining room.
  • Playing along, play / pause and next song reach that room too. The ring and single taps stay in the kitchen.
  • Hold happens when you let go, not while you press. Tap twice is quick, like a double-click.
