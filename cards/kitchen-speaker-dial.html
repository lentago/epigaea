Kitchen Speaker Dial

The round Hue dial runs the kitchen Sonos. The four buttons are marked with dots: ● ●● ●●● ●●●●

Turn the ring Clockwise is louder, back the other way is quieter. A fast spin jumps more. | All the way down is silent. There is no mute button.
Button Tap once Hold a second, let go Tap twice
● Play / pause Kitchen only (every other room drops off) Just the kitchen and dining room together, both at 25%. Anyone else drops off.
●● Volume 20% Dining room joins or leaves Everyone in the group to 20%
●●● Volume 30% Office joins or leaves Everyone in the group to 30%
●●●● Volume 40% Nothing Everyone in the group to 40%

●  tapped three times is the first floor at 25%: the kitchen, dining room and office, nobody else.

  • Joins means that room starts playing whatever the kitchen is playing. Hold the same button again and it drops off.
  • Hold happens when you let go, not while you press. Tap twice is quick, like a double-click.
  • The ring only changes the kitchen. To set every room in the group at once, tap ●●, ●●● or ●●●● twice.
  • The basement, bedroom and Roam are not on this dial. Use the Sonos app for those.
  • Speakers that are unplugged are skipped. Nothing here touches the lights.
