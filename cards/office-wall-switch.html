Office Wall Switch

The flat paddle on the wall runs all six office lamps. Press the top of it or the bottom of it.

Paddle Tap Press and hold
▲ Top Lamps on, at Bright. Tap again for the next scene. Brighter, until you let go
▼ Bottom All the lamps off Dimmer, until you let go. Stops low, never off.
Scenes, in tap order
  1. 1Bright warm, full
  2. 2Relax warm, low
  3. 3Focus cool white, full
  4. 4Amber candle
  5. 5Red candle
  6. 6Blue candle
  7. 7Red desk, blue room
  8. 8Rainbow all together
  9. 9Rainbow wave
4 to 9 flicker like a flame or drift through colours. After 9 the next tap is Bright again.
  • Turning the lamps on always starts at Bright. To get back to Bright from a flickering scene, tap the bottom, then the top.
  • Hold only works while the lamps are on. Each new scene starts at its own brightness.
  • The wireless office button steps through the same scenes. Either one picks up where the other left off.
  • During a meeting the corner lamp stays red and the switch leaves it alone. It rejoins the room when the meeting ends.
