Meeting Button

Press it when a call or meeting starts, and the house shows a red light so nobody walks in. Press it again when you are done.

Press What happens
To start Meeting on. The hallway light comes on red, and the office corner lamp turns red.
Again, to end Meeting over. The hallway light goes off. The corner lamp rejoins the other office lamps (or goes off, if they are off).
  • During a meeting the office switch and the office button still work, but they leave the corner lamp red.
  • If the button loses its Wi-Fi for more than about 10 seconds, the meeting clears on its own: the hallway light goes off and the corner lamp rejoins the room. When it reconnects the red comes back by itself if the button still has the meeting on. If not, press it again.
  • The meeting tile in the home app starts and ends a meeting too.
