Office Scene Button

The small wireless button runs all six office lamps. It has one button: press it once, press it twice, or hold it.

Press What happens
Once Lamps on, at Bright. Press once more for the next scene.
Twice, quickly All the lamps off
Hold Straight back to Bright, from any scene (turns the lamps on if they are off)
Scenes, in press order
  1. 1Bright warm, full
  2. 2Relax warm, low
  3. 3Focus cool white, full
  4. 4Amber candle
  5. 5Red candle
  6. 6Blue candle
  7. 7Red desk, blue room
  8. 8Rainbow all together
  9. 9Rainbow wave
4 to 9 flicker like a flame or drift through colours. After 9 the next press is Bright again.
  • The paddle on the office wall steps through the same scenes and picks up where this button left off. Only the paddle makes a scene brighter or dimmer.
  • In a meeting the corner lamp stays red and the button leaves it alone until the meeting ends.
