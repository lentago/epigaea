Downstairs Lights Button

The small wireless button runs the kitchen, the family room and both hallways, upstairs and down. It has one button: press it once, press it twice, or hold it.

Press What happens
Once Everything on, full brightness
Twice, quickly Everything off
Hold Everything on, low (about a quarter brightness, for evenings)
  • Everything means the three kitchen lights, the family room light and floor lamp, the downstairs and upstairs hallway lights, and the switched family room outlets.
  • Each press sets all of them the same way, whatever they were doing before. Press once to go from low back to full.
  • Nothing else in the house changes: not the office, not the outside lights, not the music.
