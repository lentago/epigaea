Kitchen Speaker Dial

The round Hue dial runs the kitchen Sonos. Turn the ring for the kitchen's volume: clockwise is louder, a fast spin jumps more, and all the way down is silent. There is no mute button.

Button Tap once Tap twice Hold a second, let go
Play / pause Next song Join the music nearby or leave
Volume 20% Group to 20% Join the dining room or leave
Volume 30% Group to 30% Join the office or leave
Volume 40% Group to 40% Nothing

Join: the kitchen plays along with music already playing there. Hold the same button again to leave; that room plays on. picks for you: the dining room first, then the office.

  • Joining a room that isn't playing does nothing. Start the music there first.
  • Playing along, play / pause and next song reach that room too. Group: this floor's rooms playing along.
  • Hold happens when you let go, not while you press. Tap twice is quick, like a double-click.
